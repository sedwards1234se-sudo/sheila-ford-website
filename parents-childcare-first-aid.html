Common questions

Which class is right for me?

Pediatric First Aid focuses on infants and children and is often required or recommended for daycare staff, nannies, and teachers. Heartsaver CPR/AED/First Aid covers CPR, AED use, and first aid basics. Tell Sheila about your situation on the form and she will recommend the right one.

Is there a class for my teenager who babysits?

Yes. Child & Babysitting Safety (CABS) is a 3 to 4 hour course for teens and new sitters covering basic safety, choking response, and knowing when to call for help.

Do daycare staff need certification?

Requirements vary by employer and by state licensing rules, so check with your employer or licensing office. Many childcare employers require or recommend Pediatric First Aid and CPR.

Can I bring a friend or family member?

Yes. Community classes are open enrollment. Register as an individual or bring a few friends or family members.

How long are the classes?

Pediatric First Aid and CABS run about 3 to 4 hours. Heartsaver CPR/AED/First Aid runs about 4 to 6 hours.
