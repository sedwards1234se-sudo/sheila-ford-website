About Sheila Ford CPR and More

★ DISABLED VETERAN-OWNED BUSINESS

Sheila Ford CPR and More LLC provides certified safety training for individuals, families, schools, and workplaces throughout Washington D.C., Maryland, and Northern Virginia. Every course is taught hands-on, with an emphasis on skills people can actually use in the moment that matters.

Known for her engaging teaching style and deep knowledge of CPR and first aid, Sheila Ford has built a reputation for helping students feel genuinely prepared to handle real emergency situations — a reputation built over years teaching in the Waldorf area.

Courses are available as open-enrollment community classes on scheduled dates, or as private and group sessions booked just for your family, team, school, or organization — including onsite training at your business or a public facility (onsite training is not available at private residences). Certification cards are issued directly through the American Red Cross, HSI, or American Heart Association upon completion.

Unlike larger multi-state training companies that dispatch a rotating roster of contractors, Sheila Ford CPR and More LLC is locally based in Waldorf, MD — you'll work directly with Sheila herself for every class, whether it's held here at home base or onsite anywhere across the DMV.
