Section 1: Participant information

Your name, contact details, course, and class date are collected on the registration form.

Section 2: Parties

This form is between the participant identified above ("Participant") and Sheila Ford CPR and More LLC, a Maryland limited liability company, its owner Sheila Ford, and its instructors, employees, and agents (collectively, "Company"). In exchange for the Company's permission to enroll in and attend the course identified above, Participant agrees to the terms of this form.

Section 3: Scope of covered activities

Participant is enrolling in a hands-on training course offered by the Company. Covered activities may include, depending on the course enrolled in: cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR), automated external defibrillator (AED) use, first aid, bleeding control, wound packing, tourniquet application, bandaging, splinting, and related emergency response skills. Physical activities include kneeling, bending, lifting, and performing repeated chest compressions on training manikins. All skills are practiced on training equipment or a consenting partner — not on real patients. This form applies to all courses offered by the Company, at any location where the Company teaches.

Section 4: Age requirement

This form applies only to participants who are 18 years of age or older. The Company does not enroll minors in this program under this form. Any person under 18 requires a separate written consent and waiver signed by a parent or legal guardian before enrolling.

By signing below, Participant confirms they are 18 years of age or older.

Section 5: Assumption of risk

Participant understands that hands-on physical training carries inherent risks, including but not limited to: muscle strain; joint or back discomfort; bruising or scrapes from training equipment; and slips, trips, or falls in the training area. Participant voluntarily chooses to participate knowing these risks exist and acknowledges that no training environment can be made entirely risk-free.

Section 6: Communicable disease and shared equipment risk

Training manikins, AED trainer devices, and other equipment are used by multiple students. The Company wipes all shared equipment with EPA-registered disinfectant wipes between each student use. Despite these precautions, shared equipment carries a small inherent risk of transmitting illness. Participant acknowledges this risk and assumes it as a condition of participation.

Section 7: Health self-certification

Participant confirms that they are physically able to participate in this course, or that they will inform the instructor of any physical limitation before class begins. Participant is not required to disclose specific medical details. Participant may modify or skip any activity, take a break, or stop at any time, and understands that doing so may affect whether they can meet course completion requirements. Participant is solely responsible for participating within their own physical limits.

Section 8: Informed consent to hands-on instruction

Participant consents to the following hands-on instruction:

  • Performing chest compressions and rescue breaths on training manikins.
  • Using AED trainer devices and practice pads.
  • Practicing bleeding control, wound packing, tourniquet application, bandaging, and splinting — which may be practiced on a consenting partner or the instructor, and which involves appropriate physical contact for that purpose.
  • Receiving instructor demonstration and hands-on correction of hand position, posture, and technique.

Participant may request to practice exclusively on a manikin or to opt out of any partner activity. The instructor will accommodate that request where reasonably possible. Participant understands that manikins are shared equipment cleaned between uses as described in Section 6.

Section 9: Release of liability

In exchange for permission to participate, and to the fullest extent permitted by Maryland law, Participant releases and agrees not to sue or hold liable the Company — including Sheila Ford CPR and More LLC, Sheila Ford, and the Company's instructors, employees, and agents — for any injury, illness, or property damage arising from Participant's participation in any Company course, including claims based on negligence.

This release extends to Participant's heirs, personal representatives, and anyone claiming through Participant.

This release applies regardless of the location where the course is taught, including the Company's own facility, a participant's workplace, a community center, or any other host site.

Section 10: Gross negligence and willful misconduct carve-out

The release in Section 9 does not apply to claims arising from the Company's gross negligence, recklessness, willful misconduct, or any claim that Maryland law does not permit a participant to waive.

Section 11: Host-site limitation

When a course is taught at a third-party or employer host site (such as a corporate office, community center, or fire station), the release in this form covers only the Company and its instructors and agents. The host site's own policies, safety obligations, and liability are entirely separate from this form. The Company makes no representation regarding the host site's safety or legal obligations.

Section 12: Emergencies and cost of care

If Participant is injured or becomes ill during class, the instructor may call 911 and provide basic first aid. Participant is solely responsible for all costs of emergency medical care, treatment, and transport. The Company has no obligation to pay for or arrange medical care beyond calling for emergency services.

Section 13: Course limitations and certification disclaimer

This course teaches emergency response skills. Completing it does not make Participant a licensed or certified medical professional, and it does not guarantee that any rescue attempt will succeed.

A course completion card is issued only if Participant meets the attendance and skills requirements of the applicable certifying organization — which may be the American Heart Association, the American Red Cross, or the Health & Safety Institute, depending on the course. The Company makes no guarantee that any employer, school, licensing board, or other entity will accept the completion card.

Section 15: Dispute resolution

Before filing any legal action, the party with a dispute must:

  1. Written notice: Send written notice of the dispute to the other party describing the claim in reasonable detail.
  2. Mediation: If the dispute is not resolved within 30 days of the written notice, the parties must attempt non-binding mediation in Charles County, Maryland before either party may file a lawsuit.

If mediation does not resolve the dispute, either party may file suit in the Circuit Court for Charles County, Maryland, as provided in Section 16.

Section 16: Governing law and venue

This form is governed by the laws of the State of Maryland. Any litigation arising from this form shall be filed exclusively in the Circuit Court for Charles County, Maryland. Both parties consent to personal jurisdiction and venue in that court.
