Section 3: Parties

This form is between the parent or legal guardian identified above ("Parent") and Sheila Ford CPR and More LLC, a Maryland limited liability company, its owner Sheila Ford, and its instructors, employees, and agents (collectively, "Company"). It concerns the enrollment of the minor identified above ("Minor") in the course identified above. In exchange for the Company's permission for the Minor to enroll in and attend the course, Parent agrees to the terms of this form.

Section 4: Parent or guardian authority

Parent confirms that Parent is 18 years of age or older and is the Minor's parent or legal guardian with legal authority to enroll the Minor in this course and to sign this form on the Minor's behalf.

Section 5: Scope of covered activities

The Minor is enrolling in a hands-on training course offered by the Company. Covered activities may include, depending on the course: cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR), automated external defibrillator (AED) use, first aid, bleeding control, bandaging, splinting, choking response, and related emergency response skills. Physical activities include kneeling, bending, lifting, and performing repeated chest compressions on training manikins. All skills are practiced on training equipment or, where noted in Section 9, with a consenting partner. They are not practiced on real patients. This form applies at any location where the Company teaches.

Section 6: Assumption of risk

Parent understands that hands-on physical training carries inherent risks, including but not limited to: muscle strain; joint or back discomfort; bruising or scrapes from training equipment; and slips, trips, or falls in the training area. Parent voluntarily chooses to enroll the Minor knowing these risks exist and acknowledges that no training environment can be made entirely risk-free.

Section 7: Communicable disease and shared equipment risk

Training manikins, AED trainer devices, and other equipment are used by multiple students. The Company wipes shared equipment with EPA-registered disinfectant wipes between each student use. Despite these precautions, shared equipment carries a small inherent risk of transmitting illness. Parent acknowledges this risk and assumes it on the Minor's behalf as a condition of participation.

Section 8: Health information and participation limits

Parent confirms that, to Parent's knowledge, the Minor is physically able to participate in this course, or that Parent will inform the instructor of any physical limitation, allergy (including latex), or condition the instructor should know about before class begins. Parent is not required to disclose specific medical details. The Minor may modify or skip any activity, take a break, or stop at any time, and Parent understands that doing so may affect whether the Minor can meet course completion requirements.

Section 9: Informed consent to hands-on instruction

Parent consents to the Minor receiving the following hands-on instruction:

  • Performing chest compressions and rescue breaths on training manikins.
  • Using AED trainer devices and practice pads.
  • Practicing bleeding control, bandaging, and splinting, which may be practiced on a consenting partner or the instructor and involves appropriate physical contact for that purpose.
  • Receiving instructor demonstration and hands-on correction of hand position, posture, and technique.

Parent may request that the Minor practice only on a manikin and not with a partner. The Company will accommodate that request where reasonably possible. Parent may check the box below to make that request now.

Section 10: Parent's release of liability

In exchange for permission for the Minor to participate, and to the fullest extent permitted by Maryland law, Parent, for Parent and Parent's heirs, personal representatives, and anyone claiming through Parent, releases and agrees not to sue or hold liable the Company, including Sheila Ford CPR and More LLC, Sheila Ford, and the Company's instructors, employees, and agents, for any injury, illness, or property damage arising from the Minor's participation in any Company course, including claims based on negligence. This includes Parent's own claims, such as claims for medical expenses and loss of services.

To the extent permitted by Maryland law, Parent also releases such claims on the Minor's behalf. Parent understands that a court may not enforce a release of a minor's own claims, and that this form is not a promise that it will be enforced as to the Minor.

Section 11: Gross negligence and willful misconduct carve-out

The release in Section 10 does not apply to claims arising from the Company's gross negligence, recklessness, willful misconduct, or any claim that Maryland law does not permit a parent or minor to waive.

Section 12: Host-site limitation

When a course is taught at a third-party host site (such as a school, community center, or fire station), the release in this form covers only the Company and its instructors and agents. The host site's own policies, safety obligations, and liability are entirely separate from this form. The Company makes no representation regarding the host site's safety or legal obligations.

Section 13: Emergencies, medical treatment, and cost of care

If the Minor is injured or becomes ill during class, the instructor may call 911 and provide basic first aid. Parent authorizes emergency medical treatment and transport for the Minor if Parent or the emergency contact above cannot be reached promptly. Parent is solely responsible for all costs of emergency medical care, treatment, and transport. The Company has no obligation to pay for or arrange medical care beyond calling for emergency services and notifying Parent.

Section 14: Supervision, conduct, and pickup

  • Parent is responsible for dropping off and picking up the Minor on time. The Company supervises the Minor only during scheduled class time.
  • A parent or guardian is welcome to stay at the class location for the duration of the class.
  • The instructor may ask a participant to leave a class for unsafe or disruptive behavior. In that case Parent will be contacted to pick up the Minor, and no refund is owed for that session.
  • Parent will make sure the Minor is told to follow instructor directions and to use equipment only as instructed.

Section 15: Course limitations and certification disclaimer

This course teaches emergency response skills. Completing it does not make the Minor a licensed or certified medical professional, and it does not guarantee that any rescue attempt will succeed.

A course completion card is issued only if the Minor meets the attendance, skills, and any age requirements of the applicable certifying organization, which may be the American Heart Association, the American Red Cross, or the Health & Safety Institute, depending on the course. The Company makes no guarantee that any employer, school, program, or other entity will accept the completion card.
