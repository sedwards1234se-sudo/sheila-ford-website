Common questions

Do you train at our office?

Yes. Mobile onsite training is available at businesses and public facilities across DC, Maryland, and Northern Virginia. It is not available at private residences.

How many people do we need?

Onsite sessions are for groups of three or more. One-on-one or small sessions for one or two people can be arranged based on need. Package pricing is for groups of 10 to 15.

What does the travel fee cover?

A flat fee per visit ($50 in Charles County and Waldorf, $75 for the rest of Maryland and DC, $100 for Northern Virginia). It covers instructor travel and transport of manikins, AED trainers, and supplies.

Will our employer or regulator accept the certification cards?

Training requirements vary by employer, industry, and jurisdiction. Check what your workplace requires, and Sheila can help you choose the right courses. Cards are issued through the American Red Cross, HSI, or American Heart Association.

How do we book?

Use the form, choose Onsite Training, and put your group size and location in the notes. Sheila will follow up to confirm scheduling and pricing.

Ready to schedule your team?

Tell us your group size and location and Sheila will confirm the date, total pricing, and next steps.
