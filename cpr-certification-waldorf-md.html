Common questions

Do I get my certification the same day?

Yes, on Instructor-Led classes. You take the full in-person class and are certified that day. Blended courses finish your online coursework first, then a 1.5 hour in-person skills check.

Which course do I need?

Healthcare providers, students, nurses, and EMTs usually need Basic Life Support (BLS). Most workplaces, schools, and the general public choose Heartsaver CPR/AED/First Aid or the Red Cross First Aid/CPR/AED. Check with your employer or school if you are unsure, or ask Sheila on the form.

Do I need any experience?

No. Heartsaver and the Red Cross First Aid/CPR/AED course require no healthcare background.

Where are classes held?

Community classes meet Saturdays at a set location that is confirmed when you register. Private and group classes are booked Monday through Friday, including onsite at businesses and public facilities (not private residences).

How do I renew my certification?

Choose the Renewal option for your course. It is a skills check for anyone with a current certification, and it costs less than the full class.
